## Safety Status: Generally Safe

Commercially packaged mozzarella cheese is **safe** to eat during pregnancy. 

According to safety guidelines from the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, soft cheeses are safe for pregnant individuals provided they are made from pasteurized milk. Commercial mozzarella undergo pasteurization—a heat-treatment process that destroys harmful pathogens like **Listeria monocytogenes**. 

Because commercially sold mozzarella (including block, shredded, and string cheese) in supermarkets is pasteurized, it carries an extremely low risk of foodborne illness.

## Nutritional Benefits for Pregnancy

Mozzarella is an excellent, nutrient-dense snack for maternal wellness:
*   **Calcium:** Supports the development of your baby’s skeletal system, bones, and teeth.
*   **Protein:** Provides essential amino acids for fetal tissue development and placenta growth.
*   **Phosphorus:** Works alongside calcium to support healthy bone structure.
*   **B Vitamins:** Helps maintain energy levels and supports cell health.

## Safe Selection and Kitchen Safety

To enjoy mozzarella cheese safely:
*   **Check the Packaging:** Verify that the label states "made with pasteurized milk." This is particularly important for fresh mozzarella balls sold in water or brine.
*   **Avoid Cross-Contamination:** When preparing salads (such as Caprese salad), ensure your cutting board and knives are clean, and wash fresh basil and tomatoes thoroughly.
*   **Keep Cold:** Store mozzarella in the refrigerator at or below **40°F (4°C)**. Keep it in its original packaging or brine.
*   **Observe Expiration Dates:** Eat fresh mozzarella within 5 to 7 days of opening. Shredded or block mozzarella can last longer but should be checked for signs of mold before consumption.
