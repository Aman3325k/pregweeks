## Safety Status: Safe to Consume

Thoroughly cooked, well-done turkey is completely **safe** and serves as an excellent lean protein source during pregnancy. 

According to safety standards from the **U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA)** and the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, turkey must reach a minimum internal temperature of **165°F (74°C)** to be safe. Reaching this temperature destroys common foodborne pathogens like *Salmonella*, *Campylobacter*, and *Listeria monocytogenes*, protecting both mother and baby from serious infections.

## Fetal Growth and Nutritional Benefits of Turkey

Turkey is a highly nutritious choice for a prenatal diet, providing essential building blocks for your pregnancy:

*   **Lean Protein:** Essential for the rapid growth of fetal tissues, placenta development, and the expansion of maternal blood volume.
*   **Selenium and Zinc:** Support your baby’s immune system development, cell division, and healthy thyroid function.
*   **Vitamin B6 and B12:** Crucial for baby’s brain and nervous system development, and help prevent neural tube defects and maternal anemia.
*   **Iron:** Supports red blood cell production, helping prevent gestational anemia and low birth weight.

## USDA Cooking and Leftover Safety Standards

To ensure turkey is safe throughout your pregnancy, you must follow these specific handling and preparation guidelines:

*   **Cook to 165°F (74°C):** Insert a digital food thermometer into the thickest part of the turkey (the inner thigh and breast), ensuring it does not touch the bone, which heats faster. Whole turkey and ground turkey must reach 165°F (74°C) before eating.
*   **Handle Leftovers with Caution:** Leftover cooked turkey is a high-risk food. The CDC warns that *Listeria monocytogenes* can grow slowly in the refrigerator, even at safe temperatures (**32°F to 40°F** / **0°C to 4°C**). 
    *   Do not eat cold leftover turkey slices straight from the refrigerator.
    *   Reheat leftover turkey until it is **steaming hot** (internal temperature of **165°F / 74°C**) right before serving.
    *   Discard any cooked turkey that has sat in the refrigerator for more than **2 to 3 days**.
*   **Avoid Raw Poultry Washing:** Do not rinse raw turkey before cooking, as this splashes water droplets containing Salmonella and Campylobacter up to 3 feet around your kitchen. Cooking is the only way to kill bacteria.
