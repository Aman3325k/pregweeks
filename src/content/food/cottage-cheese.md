## Safety Status: Safe (Pasteurized)

Commercially manufactured **cottage cheese** is completely **safe** to consume during pregnancy, provided it is made from pasteurized milk. In the United States and many other countries, federal regulations require all commercially sold cottage cheese to be pasteurized, making it a low-risk, highly nutritious choice for expectant mothers.

According to food safety guidelines from the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, the primary concern with dairy products during pregnancy is **Listeria monocytogenes**. Listeria is a bacterium that can survive at refrigerator temperatures and cause listeriosis, a severe infection that can lead to miscarriage, stillbirth, or severe neonatal illness. 

Because commercial cottage cheese undergoes pasteurization—a heat-treatment process that destroys harmful pathogens—the risk of Listeria contamination in unopened, commercial packages is extremely low.

## Nutritional Benefits

Cottage cheese is a highly recommended food for pregnancy due to its dense nutrient profile:
*   **High Protein Content:** Protein is essential for the rapid growth and development of fetal tissues, including the brain. A single cup of cottage cheese contains about 25 grams of protein.
*   **Calcium Source:** Calcium is vital for building your baby's bones and teeth, and it helps maintain maternal bone density.
*   **B Vitamins:** It is rich in vitamin B12 and riboflavin, which support energy production and nervous system health.

## Safe Handling and Storage Practices

To prevent accidental contamination after purchase, practice these food safety steps:
*   **Verify Pasteurization:** Always check the ingredient list. Look for the phrase "made from pasteurized milk" or "pasteurized."
*   **Store Properly:** Keep cottage cheese refrigerated below **40°F (4°C)**. Do not leave the container at room temperature for more than 2 hours (or 1 hour if the ambient temperature is above 90°F / 32°C).
*   **Avoid Cross-Contamination:** Always use a clean spoon to scoop cottage cheese from the tub. Never double-dip or use utensils that have touched other foods, which can introduce bacteria.
*   **Discard Promptly:** Once opened, consume the cottage cheese within **3 to 5 days**, even if the expiration date on the package is further out.
