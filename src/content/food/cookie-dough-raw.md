## Safety Status: Avoid Entirely

Raw cookie dough and cake batter must be strictly **avoided** during pregnancy. 

According to standard food safety warnings from the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, raw cookie dough presents a double hazard of foodborne illness. 

First, raw dough typically contains **raw eggs**, which carry a risk of **Salmonella enteritidis** contamination. Salmonella can cause severe gastrointestinal illness, fever, vomiting, and dehydration in pregnant individuals, which can trigger uterine contractions or preterm labor. 

Second, **raw, unbaked flour** is a significant and often overlooked source of foodborne pathogens, specifically **Escherichia coli (E. coli)** and **Salmonella**. Because flour is made from wheat grown in open fields, it can be contaminated by wild animal feces. The milling process does not kill bacteria, meaning raw flour can contain pathogens that are only destroyed by the baking process.

## Safe Alternatives & Edible Cookie Dough

You can safely satisfy cookie dough cravings with these options:
*   **Edible Cookie Dough:** Choose commercial brands of "edible cookie dough" sold in supermarkets. These products are made specifically to be eaten raw, utilizing pasteurized eggs (or no eggs) and flour that has undergone commercial heat-treatment to kill E. coli.
*   **Fully Baked Cookies:** Bake your cookie dough in the oven until the cookies are firm. The heat of the oven destroys any pathogens present in the flour and eggs.
*   **Make Safe Dough at Home:** If making edible dough at home, use a recipe that does not include eggs, and heat-treat your flour first by spreading it on a baking sheet and baking it at **350°F (176°C)** until the internal temperature of the flour reaches **160°F (71°C)**.

## Kitchen Hygiene and Baking Safety

When baking at home, practice good food safety:
*   **Wash Hands and Surfaces:** Wash your hands, bowls, utensils, and countertops thoroughly with warm water and soap after handling raw flour or raw eggs.
*   **Do Not Taste Raw Batter:** Keep children and pregnant individuals from tasting raw cake batter, cookie dough, pancake batter, or raw bread dough.
*   **Keep Raw Flour Sealed:** Store flour in a clean, airtight container away from ready-to-eat foods to prevent cross-contamination.
