## Safety Status: Avoid Entirely (No Safe Limit)

**Alcohol** (including wine, beer, cider, and distilled spirits) must be strictly **avoided** during all stages of pregnancy.

According to warnings from the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, the **U.S. Surgeon General**, the **American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG)**, and the **World Health Organization (WHO)**, there is no known safe amount of alcohol to drink during pregnancy, nor is there any safe time or safe type of alcohol.

When a pregnant individual drinks alcohol, it enters the bloodstream and easily crosses the placental barrier. 

Because the baby’s liver is still developing and cannot process alcohol, the fetus absorbs the substance, leading to similar blood alcohol concentrations as the mother. 

Alcohol is a potent teratogen (a substance that causes birth defects) that directly interferes with fetal cellular division and tissue oxygenation, particularly in the brain.

## Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorders (FASD)

Maternal alcohol consumption is the leading preventable cause of birth defects and developmental disabilities worldwide. Exposure in the womb can result in **Fetal Alcohol Spectrum Disorders (FASD)**, which encompass a range of permanent physical and mental disabilities:
*   **Brain Damage and Cognitive Impairment:** Leading to learning disabilities, low IQ, poor coordination, and executive functioning issues.
*   **Behavioral Challenges:** Problems with attention (often misdiagnosed as ADHD), impulse control, and social interactions.
*   **Physical Anomalies:** Distinctive facial features (such as a smooth philtrum, thin upper lip, and small eye openings), low birth weight, and poor muscle tone.
*   **Organ Defects:** Increased risk of congenital heart, kidney, or skeletal defects.

## Safe and Festive Mocktail Ideas

You can safely celebrate special occasions with these alcohol-free options:
*   **Sparkling Fruit Spritzers:** Mix sparkling water with a splash of 100% pasteurized cranberry, pomegranate, or apple juice, garnished with fresh mint or lime slices.
*   **Ginger-Lime Mocktail:** Combine alcohol-free ginger beer (ensure it is ginger soda, not alcoholic beer) with fresh lime juice and club soda.
*   **Verify 'Non-Alcoholic' Labels:** Read labels carefully. In the U.S. and Europe, beverages labeled "non-alcoholic" can legally contain up to **0.5% ABV**. Only choose products that explicitly state **0.0% ABV** or "alcohol-free," and discuss them with your healthcare provider first.
