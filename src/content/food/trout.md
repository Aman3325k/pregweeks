## Safety Status: Safe (Fully Cooked)

Fully cooked **freshwater trout** (such as rainbow trout) is **safe** and highly recommended during pregnancy. 

The **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)** place farmed trout on their "Best Choices" list. It is a clean, low-mercury fish that provides critical nutrients for fetal growth.

The primary food safety concern with freshwater fish is **cooking temperature**. Raw or undercooked trout can harbor freshwater parasites, such as tapeworms (*Diphyllobothrium* species), as well as bacteria like **Listeria monocytogenes** and **Salmonella**. 

Thoroughly cooking the fish to the proper temperature destroys these biological hazards, making it safe for prenatal consumption.

## Farmed vs. Wild Trout Safety

Pregnant individuals should be mindful of where the trout was sourced:
*   **Farmed Trout (Highly Safe):** Most trout sold in grocery stores and restaurants is farm-raised in freshwater raceways. These fish have low, consistent mercury levels and are safe to consume up to 2-3 times per week.
*   **Wild-Caught Trout (Check Advisories):** If you or someone you know catches wild trout from local streams, rivers, or lakes, you must check local fish consumption advisories. Some bodies of water contain elevated levels of industrial pollutants like **polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs)** or mercury. If no local advisory is available, limit consumption of wild-caught fish to **6 ounces per week** and do not eat any other fish that week.

## Nutritional Benefits

Trout is an excellent dietary addition during pregnancy:
*   **Rich in DHA Omega-3s:** Essential for the development of your baby’s brain and eyes.
*   **High-Quality Protein:** Supports cellular growth and blood volume expansion.
*   **Vitamin D and Potassium:** Vital for bone mineralization and blood pressure regulation.

## Safe Preparation and Cooking Guidelines

Ensure safety during preparation:
*   **Cook to 145°F (63°C):** Bake, grill, or pan-sear trout until the internal temperature reaches 145°F. The flesh should be opaque throughout and flake easily with a fork.
*   **Hygiene:** Wash hands, cutting boards, and knives with hot soapy water after handling raw fish to prevent cross-contamination.
