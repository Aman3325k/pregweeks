## Safety Status: Generally Safe

Hard cheeses, such as Cheddar (along with Parmesan, Swiss, Gouda, and Provolone), are completely **safe** to eat during pregnancy. 

According to guidelines from the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, hard cheeses do not pose a significant risk of *Listeria monocytogenes* contamination. During the manufacturing process, hard cheeses are pressed to remove excess whey, resulting in a low moisture content. 

Listeria bacteria require a moist, low-acid environment to survive and multiply. Because hard cheeses are dry and undergo an aging process that increases acidity, they are naturally hostile to bacterial growth. Even Cheddar made from unpasteurized milk is considered safe by food safety authorities due to this low moisture level, though commercially pasteurized Cheddar is always recommended for maximum safety.

## Nutritional Benefits for Pregnancy

Including pasteurized Cheddar in your prenatal diet provides essential nutrients:
*   **Calcium:** Critical for developing your baby’s bones and teeth. If maternal calcium intake is insufficient, your body will draw calcium from your own bones to support the fetus.
*   **High-Quality Protein:** Supports the growth of the fetus, placenta, and maternal breast and uterine tissues.
*   **Zinc:** Supports cell division, cellular growth, and immune function.
*   **Vitamin B12:** Essential for healthy red blood cell production and nervous system function.

## Safe Handling & Storage

To keep your Cheddar cheese fresh and safe:
*   **Refrigerate Properly:** Keep Cheddar wrapped tightly in cheese paper or plastic wrap and store it in your refrigerator at or below **40°F (4°C)**.
*   **Cut Off Superficial Mold:** If a block of Cheddar develops a small spot of mold, you do not have to discard the whole block. Cut off at least 1 inch around and below the mold spot, making sure the knife does not touch the mold, and consume the remaining clean cheese.
*   **Check Ingredient Labels:** When buying pre-shredded or block Cheddar, verify that the package lists "pasteurized milk" to ensure maximum safety.
