## Safety Status: Safe (Fully Cooked)

Fully cooked **cod** is **safe** and highly recommended for consumption during pregnancy. 

Under dietary guidelines from the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)**, cod is classified on the "Best Choices" list. It has very low levels of methylmercury, making it a safe seafood option to consume regularly.

However, you must avoid **raw or undercooked cod**. Raw white fish can carry marine parasites (such as cod worms or nematodes) and bacteria (such as **Listeria monocytogenes** and **Salmonella**). 

Listeria can cause listeriosis, which carries severe risks for pregnant women, including miscarriage and neonatal infection. Thoroughly cooking the cod kills any potential parasites or pathogens, rendering it safe.

## Nutritional Benefits of Cod

Cod is an excellent, low-fat source of nutrition during pregnancy:
*   **High-Quality Lean Protein:** Helps build the baby's muscles, tissues, and organs without adding excess saturated fats to the maternal diet.
*   **Iodine Source:** Iodine is a critical nutrient for pregnancy. It is essential for producing maternal thyroid hormones, which regulate the development of the fetal brain and nervous system.
*   **B Vitamins:** Rich in Vitamin B12 and B6, which support red blood cell production and nervous system health.
*   **Selenium:** An antioxidant mineral that protects cells and supports thyroid function.

## Safe Cooking and Handling Guidelines

Follow these safety steps when preparing cod:
*   **Cook to 145°F (63°C):** Cook cod until it reaches an internal temperature of 145°F. The meat should change from translucent to opaque white and flake easily with a fork.
*   **Avoid Raw Preparations:** Do not consume cod in raw or partially cooked dishes, such as ceviche or raw sushi, unless the fish was commercial flash-frozen under specific anti-parasitic protocols (even then, cooked is much safer).
*   **Safe Portioning:** As a "Best Choice" fish, you can safely consume **8 to 12 ounces (2 to 3 servings)** of cod per week.
