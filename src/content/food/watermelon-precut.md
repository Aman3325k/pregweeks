## Safety Status: Consume with Caution (Moderate)

Pre-cut watermelon, cantaloupe, and honeydew melons sold in plastic containers or deli cups should be consumed **with caution** during pregnancy. 

Health agencies, including the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)**, list pre-cut melons as moderate-to-high-risk foods for foodborne infections. The main threat is **Listeria monocytogenes**, which has been linked to numerous multi-state outbreaks associated with supermarket pre-cut fruit displays.

## The Mechanism of Contamination

Watermelons grow directly on the ground, where their tough, porous rinds come into contact with soil, manure, and irrigation water. 

*   **Rind Pathogens:** Bacteria like Listeria and Salmonella can easily attach to the rind. Since the rind is not eaten, agricultural processors and retail stores often do not wash it thoroughly before cutting.
*   **Mechanical Cross-Contamination:** When a retail worker or automated machine slices a knife through the unwashed rind of a watermelon, the blade acts as a vehicle, transferring surface bacteria directly into the internal flesh of the fruit.
*   **Ideal Growing Medium:** Watermelon flesh is neutral in acidity and high in water and sugar, creating an ideal medium for Listeria to multiply, even under refrigeration. Listeriosis in pregnant individuals is highly dangerous; it can cross the placenta, leading to miscarriage, stillbirth, premature labor, or severe newborn infections.

## Safe Melon Alternatives and Preparation

To enjoy watermelon safely:

*   **Purchase Whole Watermelons:** The safest option is to buy a whole, uncut watermelon.
*   **Scrub the Rind at Home:** Before slicing, place the whole melon under cold, running water. Use a clean vegetable brush to scrub the outer rind thoroughly. This physically removes dirt, debris, and any bacteria, preventing them from transferring to the flesh when you cut it.
*   **Use Clean Utensils:** Use a clean cutting board and a clean, sharp knife. Do not use a knife that has recently cut raw meat without sanitizing it first.
*   **Store Properly:** Slice the melon, place it in an airtight container, and store it in the refrigerator at or below **40°F (4°C)**.
*   **Consume Quickly:** Eat the cut watermelon within **2 to 3 days**. Throw away any cut melon that has sat in the refrigerator longer, or has been left at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
