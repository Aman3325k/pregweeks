## Safety Status: Safe to Consume

Peanuts and peanut butter are entirely **safe** and highly recommended to consume during pregnancy, provided the mother does not have a diagnosed peanut allergy. 

In the past, outdated advice suggested that pregnant individuals avoid highly allergenic foods like peanuts to prevent allergies in their children. However, extensive clinical studies and updated guidelines from the **American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)**, the **American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG)**, and the **National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID)** have reversed this recommendation. 

Current medical consensus confirms that avoiding peanuts during pregnancy is unnecessary and that consuming them may actually help build the baby's immune tolerance, reducing the risk of childhood peanut allergies.

## Maternal and Fetal Nutritional Benefits

Peanuts are a nutrient-dense food that provides several key benefits:

*   **Folate (Vitamin B9):** Peanuts are a good natural source of folate, which is essential for DNA synthesis, cell division, and the prevention of neural tube defects (such as spina bifida) in the early stages of fetal development.
*   **High-Quality Protein:** Essential for supporting the growth of fetal tissues, uterine development, and blood supply expansion.
*   **Monounsaturated and Polyunsaturated Fats:** These healthy fats support the development of your baby’s brain, eyes, and cellular structures.
*   **Magnesium and Potassium:** Minerals that help maintain healthy blood pressure levels and can help reduce the frequency of pregnancy-related leg cramps.
*   **Niacin (Vitamin B3):** Helps support maternal skin health, digestion, and energy production.

## Safe Selection and Dietary Tips

To enjoy peanuts safely in your pregnancy diet:

*   **Check for Mold (Aflatoxins):** Peanuts grow underground and can be susceptible to *Aspergillus* mold, which produces **aflatoxins** (toxic compounds). In developed countries, commercial peanuts are strictly tested and regulated for aflatoxins. To ensure safety, buy reputable commercial brands and avoid raw peanuts that show signs of discoloration or mold.
*   **Natural is Best:** When purchasing peanut butter, look for "natural" varieties where the ingredients list is simply "peanuts" or "peanuts and salt." Avoid brands with added hydrogenated oils (trans fats) and high-fructose corn syrup.
*   **Portion Moderation:** Peanut butter is highly calorie-dense. A standard serving size is **2 tablespoons** (about 190 calories), which is sufficient to gain the nutritional benefits without contributing to excessive weight gain.
