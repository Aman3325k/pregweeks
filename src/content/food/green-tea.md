## Safety Status: Moderate (Limit Consumption)

**Green tea** and **matcha** are safe to consume during pregnancy, but only in **moderation**. 

A standard 8-ounce cup of green tea contains **30 to 50 mg of caffeine**, while a serving of matcha (made from stone-ground green tea leaves) contains about **70 mg of caffeine**. The **American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG)** recommends keeping total daily caffeine below **200 mg** to prevent risks of low birth weight.

However, green tea has a second, pregnancy-specific caution: it contains **epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG)**, a powerful antioxidant. 

In clinical studies, EGCG has been shown to temporarily block the action of **dihydrofolate reductase (DHFR)**, an enzyme essential for cellular absorption of **folate (folic acid)**. 

Because folate is critical—especially in the first trimester—to prevent neural tube defects such as spina bifida, consuming large quantities of green tea can reduce folate bio-availability.

## Tannins and Iron Absorption

Like black tea, green tea contains **tannins**, which are astringent polyphenols:
*   Tannins bind to non-heme iron (plant-based iron) in your digestive tract, preventing your body from absorbing it.
*   To avoid worsening pregnancy-related anemia, do not drink green tea at the same time you take your prenatal vitamins or eat iron-rich meals. Allow at least **1 to 2 hours** between green tea consumption and iron intake.

## Safe Intake Recommendations

To enjoy green tea and matcha safely during your pregnancy:
*   **Limit to 1-2 Cups Daily:** This keeps your caffeine intake low and minimizes the inhibitory effect of EGCG on folate absorption.
*   **Decaf Options:** Choose decaffeinated green tea, preferably processed using the non-chemical carbon dioxide or water methods.
*   **Avoid Concentrated Extracts:** Do not consume green tea dietary supplements, pills, or weight-loss extracts, as these contain extremely high, unregulated concentrations of EGCG and caffeine that are dangerous for fetal development.
