## Safety Status: Safe to Consume

Thoroughly washed lettuce and salad greens (including romaine, butterhead, spinach, kale, and arugula) are completely **safe** and highly recommended during pregnancy. 

While raw vegetables can carry pathogens from soil or water, proper washing removes these hazards. The **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and the **Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC)** state that washing leafy vegetables under running tap water is an effective physical method to dislodge soil, bacteria, and parasites, rendering them safe for pregnant individuals.

## Prenatal Benefits: Folate, Fiber, and Vitamins

Leafy greens are an essential part of a healthy prenatal diet, providing crucial nutrients:

*   **Folate (Vitamin B9):** Folate is critical during the early weeks of pregnancy to support neural tube development and prevent congenital birth defects such as spina bifida. Leafy greens like spinach and romaine are excellent natural sources of folate.
*   **Dietary Fiber:** Progesterone levels during pregnancy slow down the digestive system, making constipation a common concern. The fiber in salad greens helps promote healthy digestion, prevents constipation, and supports maternal gut health.
*   **Vitamin K:** Vital for proper blood clotting and supports bone health in both mother and baby.
*   **Vitamins A and C:** Boost maternal immune function and promote healthy skin and tissue growth.
*   **Hydration:** Greens have a high water content, aiding overall hydration, which is necessary to support increased blood volume and amniotic fluid levels.

## Safe Washing and Storage Guidelines

To ensure your salad greens remain safe and fresh:

*   **Wash Under Running Tap Water:** Separate the lettuce leaves and rinse each one thoroughly under cold, running tap water. The physical friction of running water is the most effective way to remove dirt, bacteria, and parasites. Avoid soaking greens in a bowl or sink, as this can trap and spread bacteria.
*   **Skip Chemical Washes:** The FDA does not recommend using commercial produce washes, soaps, or detergents, as these can leave chemical residues on the leaves. Clean water is sufficient and safe.
*   **Dry with Care:** Dry the washed leaves with a clean paper towel or spin them in a clean salad spinner. Removing excess moisture prevents bacteria from multiplying during storage.
*   **Keep Chilled:** Store washed greens in a clean container in the refrigerator set at or below **40°F (4°C)**. Consume within a few days to ensure freshness.
*   **Clean Prep Tools:** Wash your hands, cutting boards, knives, and salad spinners with hot, soapy water before and after preparing salad greens to prevent cross-contamination from raw meats.
