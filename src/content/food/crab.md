## Safety Status: Safe if Fully Cooked

Fully cooked crab is classified as **safe** and is featured on the **U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA)** and **Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)** "Best Choices" list. 

Pregnant individuals can safely consume up to **8 to 12 ounces (2 to 3 servings) of crab per week**. Because crab is low on the marine food chain and has a short lifespan, it accumulates very little methylmercury in its tissues, making it an excellent, low-risk seafood option. 

However, you must strictly avoid **raw or undercooked crab** (such as raw crab claws or uncooked sushi preparations) due to the risk of foodborne bacteria and parasites. Cooking crab until the meat is opaque and firm destroys any potential pathogens.

## Nutritional Benefits for Pregnancy

Crab is highly nutritious and supports fetal growth:
*   **Zinc & Iron:** Zinc supports cell division, DNA synthesis, and immune system function. Iron helps prevent maternal anemia and supports the expanding blood volume.
*   **Protein:** Provides the foundational building blocks for fetal organs, muscles, and the placenta.
*   **Vitamin B12:** Essential for neurological development and healthy red blood cell production.
*   **Omega-3 Fatty Acids (DHA/EPA):** Contributes to the development of your baby’s brain and visual systems.

## Safe Consumption Guidelines

To eat crab safely during pregnancy:
*   **Cook Thoroughly:** If cooking fresh crab at home, steam, boil, or bake it until the internal temperature reaches **145°F (63°C)**, or until the shell turns bright red and the meat is completely white and opaque.
*   **Avoid the Tomalley:** Do not eat the tomalley (the yellowish green hepatopancreas or "crab paste" found inside the body cavity). The tomalley is an organ that filters waste and can accumulate high levels of chemical contaminants and natural marine toxins.
*   **Skip Raw Seafood buffets:** Avoid crab salads or cold crab dishes from open buffets or salad bars due to the risk of cross-contamination and temperature abuse.
*   **Verify Imitation Crab:** Imitation crab is cooked surimi (pollock) and is safe to eat. However, check that it is refrigerated properly and consumed before its expiration date.
