## Safety Status: Generally Safe

Commercial pasteurized milk (including whole milk, low-fat milk, skim milk, and organic milk) is **GENERALLY SAFE** to consume throughout all trimesters of pregnancy. 

The safety of commercial milk is due to the process of **pasteurization**. Pasteurization involves heating the raw milk to a specific temperature (typically 161°F / 72°C for at least 15 seconds) to destroy pathogens. This process kills bacteria such as *Listeria monocytogenes*, *Salmonella*, *Escherichia coli (E. coli)*, and *Campylobacter* while maintaining the milk's nutritional values. 

In contrast, **raw (unpasteurized) milk is unsafe** and must be avoided entirely during pregnancy. Raw milk can harbor these same pathogens, which can cause severe foodborne infections, potentially leading to miscarriage or fetal damage.

## Nutritional Benefits for Baby & Mother

Pasteurized milk is a nutritional powerhouse for pregnancy, providing several key building blocks for fetal development:
*   **Calcium:** Calcium is essential for building your baby's skeletal structure, bones, and teeth. If you do not consume enough calcium, your body will draw it from your own bones to support the baby, raising your risk of future bone density loss.
*   **Vitamin D:** Helps your body absorb calcium efficiently and supports fetal immune system development.
*   **Protein:** Provides amino acids that support the rapid cell growth of the fetus and the expansion of maternal uterine and breast tissues.
*   **Phosphorus and B Vitamins:** Support energy metabolism and cellular function.

## Safe Preparation & Storage Tips

To ensure your milk remains completely safe, follow these storage rules:
*   **Temperature Control:** Keep milk refrigerated below **40°F (4°C)**. Do not store milk on the refrigerator door, as opening the door exposes it to warm air, raising the temperature and speeding up bacterial spoilage. Keep it on a middle or bottom shelf instead.
*   **Check Labels:** Always look for the word "pasteurized" on the label, especially when purchasing organic, specialty, or local farm milks.
*   **Consume Promptly:** Drink milk within 7 days of opening, and discard any milk that has sat at room temperature for more than 2 hours.
*   **Check the Seal:** Avoid purchasing milk containers with broken safety seals.
